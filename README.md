# Skill-Bridge

A platform connecting skilled individuals and opportunities.

## Overview
Skill-Bridge is designed to bridge the gap between talented professionals looking to showcase their expertise and clients or organizations seeking specific skills. 

## Key Features
* **Profile Showcase:** Allow skilled individuals to highlight their experience and portfolios.
* **Opportunity Matching:** Connect users with relevant gigs, jobs, or projects.
* **Seamless Interface:** Built for quick navigation and ease of use.

## Vision
To empower freelancers, artisans, and professionals by providing a transparent and accessible space to grow their careers and find meaningful work.
